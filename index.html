I
The instrument

She listens
in five languages.

Psychology does not happen outside culture. Families, silences and the words available for feeling all differ from one language to the next.

ascoltareescucharécouterouvirlisten to listen — Italian, Spanish, French, Portuguese, English

Patients communicate the most difficult things through small distinctions — a choice of word, a shift in tone, a reference that only makes sense inside one culture. Being able to follow a person into their own language is not a convenience. It is the work.

In practice

Since 1985

Private clinical psychology practice in New York, alongside teaching, supervision and scholarship. Her multilingual work has included programmes at NYU Bellevue Hospital where language skills mattered clinically.

II
The path

Torino. Rio.
New York.

The geography of her career tells its own story. Italy gave her an undergraduate education. Brazil gave her graduate study and her first teaching post. The United States gave her a doctorate and four decades of clinical work.

Her academic accomplishments drew support from the Brazilian government twice: a federal CAPES scholarship for her master’s in 1970, and a second award several years later for doctoral study abroad.

III
Teaching and supervision

Her reach is
larger than her practice.

Psychotherapists are formed by study, but they are made by supervision — by talking their clinical work through with someone who has done it longer.

The multiplier

Seventeen years of supervision

Every clinician she supervised carried something of it into their own consulting room. Her influence runs through patients she has never met.

From 1974

She has been teaching for as long as she has been practising.
